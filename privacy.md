# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

Racing Shadow Data Bridge is a personal, non-commercial research application used by its owner.

## Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's consent, the application may use Gmail read-only permission to retrieve racing-related emails, message metadata and relevant attachments. It may also use Google Sheets to read or record the owner's private racing research. Email data is used only for these research purposes.

## Data storage and sharing

The application may process information in GitHub Actions and store results in private, owner-controlled Google Sheets or files. OAuth credentials are kept in private credential stores or encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, not published on this website. Google and GitHub provide technical infrastructure for these functions.

Gmail information is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising. The application does not send or delete emails, place bets, or process payments.

## Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke Google authorisation, remove stored automation credentials, and delete research records. Google and GitHub may retain operational records subject to their policies.
